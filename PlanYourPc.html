Plan Your First PC Build

Planning your PC build can help you choose components that fit your budget and needs.

Getting Started

Before building a PC, it is important to understand what you want to use it for and how much money you are willing to spend. Different types of computers require different components depending on their intended use.

The form below will help you organize your preferences and think about what you need from your first PC. This is a planning exercise and does not save or submit your information.

PC Build Planning Form

What's Next?

Once you have an idea of your budget and what you want to use your PC for, you can begin researching components that meet your needs.

Visit the Choosing Parts page to learn more about different component options and compatibility. You can also use PCPartPicker to compare parts and organize a potential build.
