Understanding PC Components

CPU

The CPU, or central processing unit, is responsible for processing instructions and performing calculations for your computer. The CPU you choose can have a major impact on the overall performance of your PC.

GPU

The GPU, or graphics processing unit, is responsible for creating the images that appear on your monitor. A powerful GPU is especially important for gaming, video editing, and other programs that use a lot of graphics processing.

Motherboard

The motherboard connects the different components of your computer together. When choosing a motherboard, it is important to make sure it is compatible with your CPU, memory, storage, and other components.

RAM

RAM is short-term memory that your computer uses while programs are running. Having enough RAM helps your computer run multiple programs at the same time without slowing down.

Storage

Storage is where your operating system, games, programs, and personal files are kept. SSDs are commonly used in modern computers because they are much faster than traditional hard drives.

Power Supply

The power supply provides electricity to all of the components inside your computer. It is important to choose a reliable power supply that provides enough wattage for the components in your build.

PC Case

The case holds and protects all of your computer's components. Cases come in different sizes and designs, so you should make sure your case has enough room for your motherboard, graphics card, cooling system, and other parts.
